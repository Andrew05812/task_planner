**How to read the columns.** *Line* is the line the sentence sat on in `01b4438`, the build Stage 14 shipped, taken from the hunk header. *Written into* is the call the sentence was an argument of. *Became* is the resource id the same hunk introduced; `—` means the hunk put no resource in that place, and the row says why instead. *Reader sees it* is read off *Became*, not off *Written into*: a sentence that became a resource became one because a screen prints it, and a named constant that was deleted was machine text. Deciding it from the removed line alone would be wrong — a call split over three lines leaves its sink on a line no diff of a single line can show.

## What became of them

The table answers the question it was built to answer: of the lines that carried a sentence, 45 sit on a path a reader sees, 4 were named constants the pass deleted rather than translated (`READ_FAILED`, `PASS_FAILED`, `EMAIL_TAKEN`, `FALLBACK_ERROR` — a state that carries a resource id has no need of a constant at all), and exactly one row shows `—` for another reason: `SyncStatus.Failed(e.message ?: "Sync failed")`, where the fallback was dropped because the screen was already printing its own label (`sync_state_failed`) and the machine text has nowhere to go but the log.

The change those lines add up to is measured elsewhere rather than restated here: the ledger in `docs/LOCALIZATION.md` carries the site and resource counts, and `HardcodedFailureTextTest` is the gate that keeps them — three rules (a fallback to a literal, a sink handed a literal or a named constant, a sink handed an exception's text) with a ceiling of five, each of the five named in the gate with the reason it is a diagnostic rather than copy. Its ceiling went from 29 to 5 by fixing the twenty-four, not by raising the ceiling.
