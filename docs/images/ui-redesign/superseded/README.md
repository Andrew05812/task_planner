# Superseded frames

These are real captures of this app, taken on the project's own emulator on **2026-10-30**, and they are kept
because they are the only photographs of the screens as they were before the editor and Russian-width work of
2026-10-31 landed. They are **not** evidence about the build the Stage 14 proof is about.

The distinction is a matter of bytes, not of dates. The build under proof is
`app-debug.apk` with sha256 `08838047e26e0d16c2ddd56f1e5fac9d3887c99cde480ac0307f90339e0936fc`, assembled
2026-10-31 19:13. Everything in `../after/s14/` was photographed from those bytes; everything here was
photographed from an earlier build, before two changes that these frames are routinely cited for:

- `6e0d2e2` — the plan grid and the footer that give a Russian word its room, which changed the layout several
  of these frames show;
- `a01dff4` — the tab that owns a screen carrying the reader back to it, which is behaviour no frame here can
  demonstrate because it did not exist when they were taken.

They were moved here on 2026-11-01 rather than deleted, and rather than left in `after/` beside the current
frames: a directory that mixes two builds answers a question about one build with a set of pictures that
cannot answer it, and the earlier matrix did exactly that until the provenance check was put into the capture
script. The 28 frames this directory replaces are being re-shot from the verified build; until they exist,
the Russian and 320dp columns of the Stage 14 matrix are unproven, and `docs/LOCALIZATION.md` and
`docs/PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md` §14.15 say so.
